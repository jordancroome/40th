02Getting to the track

One flight,
one bus, one boat.

Monday is the tight day. If we miss the 8:30am bus, we're driving ourselves to Port Arthur. If we miss the 2pm boat, we lose the hike. So we all take the same early flight.

Recommended flight
Jetstar JQ701 · MEL 6:00am → HBA 7:20am · Mon 10 May

The only Melbourne flight that reliably makes the 8:30am bus. Carry your pack on if you can — the bus won't wait for bags or delays.

Not flying from Melbourne?
Fly in Sunday 9 May

From Sydney, Brisbane or anywhere else, no Monday flight lands in time. Fly in Sunday night, stay in town, and catch the same bus at 7:45am from Pennicott's Hobart office.

Pennicott bus
Booking now — Jordan will confirm

About $40pp each way. Jordan is booking the group, so tick the bus questions below so he books you on.

Sun 9 MayOut-of-towners
Fly in the night before

Only if you're not flying from Melbourne. Stay somewhere central so the 7:45am bus is an easy walk.

Mon 10 MayDay 1
Airport → bus → boat → first hut
  • 6:00amJQ701 departs Melbourne
  • 7:20amLand in Hobart. Bags off, coffee in hand.
  • 7:45amBus leaves Pennicott's Hobart office (Sunday-night people)
  • 8:30amBus picks up at Hobart Airport. It won't wait for late flights.
  • 9:45amArrive Port Arthur Historic Site. Check in at the Three Capes office, lunch, last-minute repack.
  • 2:00pmBoat from Port Arthur to Denmans Cove, then 4km to Surveyors hut

Plan B if JQ701 is late or cancelled: the boat isn't until 2pm and Port Arthur is about 90 minutes' drive. Anyone landing by about 11am still makes it in a hire car. Shout in WhatsApp the moment a delay shows up and we'll split cars.

Tue–WedDays 2–3

11km on Tuesday. 19km on Wednesday out to The Blade and back to Retakunna. Drop your pack at the junction for the side trip.

Thu 13 MayDay 4

  • 2:30pmShuttle from Fortescue Bay to Port Arthur (included in your pass)
  • 4:45pmPennicott bus from Port Arthur
  • ~6:45pmBack in Hobart. Hot shower, then a pub.
