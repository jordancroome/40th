03Where to stay

Book your
own bed.

Stay near the waterfront and you can walk to everything. I'm at MACq 01. Rates below are rough guides, so check live prices for those dates. May is quiet, but don't leave it too late.

~$200 a night
Customs House Hotel · Hotel Grand Chancellor

Customs House is on the waterfront, with a pub downstairs. The Grand Chancellor is big, easy, and has room for families.

~$300 a night
Lenna of Hobart · Henry Jones Art Hotel

Lenna is in Salamanca. The Henry Jones is in old jam-factory warehouses on the wharf.

$400+ a night
MACq 01 · The Tasman

Waterfront and fancy. MACq 01 entry rooms run from roughly $340–360.
